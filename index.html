Before you trust AI with your calls.

You should be skeptical. Your phone line is revenue infrastructure. These are the questions worth asking.

Will callers know it’s AI?

The goal is not to trick people. The goal is an immediate, natural and useful experience. Disclosure language can be configured based on your preference and applicable requirements.

What happens when it doesn’t know something?

Fallback and escalation rules are defined before launch. The receptionist can collect details, transfer, route the issue or create a callback task instead of guessing.

Can it use our existing number?

In many setups, yes. Your existing number can forward into the receptionist workflow so customers keep calling the number they already know.

Can it book into our calendar?

Yes, when your scheduling stack supports the required integration. Availability, buffers, service types and booking rules are mapped around your process.

What if we have multiple teams or locations?

Calls can be routed based on service area, location, department, caller type, urgency, schedule or other business rules.

Is this just software access?

No. UnitDispatch is positioned as a done-for-you service: workflow design, configuration, testing and ongoing optimization.
