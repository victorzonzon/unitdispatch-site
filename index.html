Before you trust AI with your phone line

Skepticism is reasonable.

Your calls are too important for a generic script. These are the questions business owners should ask before putting any AI on their number.

Prefer a human? Call +1 469 825 4387 ↗
Will callers know they are speaking with AI?+

Disclosure requirements vary by jurisdiction and use case. UnitDispatch can be configured to introduce itself appropriately, and we recommend clear disclosure where required or appropriate.

What happens if the AI does not know an answer?+

It should not bluff. The receptionist can follow fallback rules: collect context, transfer the call, create a follow-up, or tell the caller what will happen next.

Can it transfer urgent calls to a real person?+

Yes. Routing and escalation logic can be configured around urgency, business hours, caller type, service category, and other criteria you define.

Can it work with my existing number?+

In many setups, yes. The exact configuration depends on your current phone system and desired routing. We map the safest deployment path before going live.

Can it book appointments?+

Yes, when your booking workflow and calendar rules are compatible. We design the receptionist around the appointment process you want callers to follow.

Is this a DIY software subscription?+

No. UnitDispatch is positioned as a done-for-you implementation service. We design the call logic, configure the receptionist, test it, and refine the operating flow around your business.
